# Security

If you find a document, pin, or reply that `verifyInstances`, `sealRequest`, or `openReply` accepts when it should refuse it, report it privately. Examples are an attestation document that AWS did not sign, a revoked pin that still passes, or a reply that opens under the wrong key. Do not open a public issue.

Report it through this repository's **Security** tab, under **Report a vulnerability**, or by email to hello@trymorse.ai. Say which tag you looked at and how to reproduce the problem.

We tell you what we found, and when a fix ships we credit you in its release unless you ask us not to.
